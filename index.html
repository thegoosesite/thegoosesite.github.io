Welcome

A site dedicated to waterfowl... not just geese! Our main species we focus on are geese (obviously), ducks, swans, and sometimes screamers but they rarely make an appearance.

Our site used to have a fictionalized deity which we removed but still shows in our graphic novel. If you are religious, we highly recommend refraining from reading the graphic novel.

Erduition

Some lore to kickstart your day.

May contain religious references

This is the Goosiverse. It is an altered timeline of the one we live in and is completely fictional. The Goose was spawned in space at around 9,000 BCE. He modeled and created Earth and all of the other planets in the system. But Earth was special... it had the potential for other forms of life. The Goose spawned in other beings known as Cow and Bull. Once they were created, they explored earth and found a conniving being known as Sinner Shark. Sinner Shark had a deal that he offered to the two recently created beings. Burn the entirety of Earth in exchange for rule on Mars. They agreed and poured lava on the planet. The Goose was outranged so he ripped their skin off and put them in a cage inside of the Earth's core for them to burn forever. But then...
