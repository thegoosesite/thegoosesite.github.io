About Us

The Goose Site was founded in May 2026 in a Python programming class. There was a running joke in said class about Geese. Absolutely no one knows the origin of this little gag (or you could say "gaggle") but it got so bad there were presentations made and then a site! This webpage was the original goose site, now known as The Goose Site Legacy. The site would continue to get maintained until in August of that same year we created this site, then The Goose Site: Relaunch. The old site (viewable here, requires passcode) had a very tacky design and we wanted something a lot more modern and polished. The new Goose Site was officially launched on August 24th, 2026; three weeks ahead of schedule.
