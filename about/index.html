About

What started as a small gaggle in a Python programming class quickly spiraled out of control. Nobody in that room quite remembers how the running obsession began, but before long, it inspired full student presentations and eventually a fully dedicated website.


The original May 2026 project now known as The Goose Site Legacy was a password-protected surprise for one of the students in the class who disliked geese. The subject had 10 simple pages and was quite small. By August of that same year, the developers were seeking a modern, clean, and glassy design.


The new goose site officially debuted on August 24, 2026, delivering a polished modern experience three weeks ahead of schedule.
