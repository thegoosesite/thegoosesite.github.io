Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the team redo the site?

  • The old site was tacky and unprofessional.

  • We needed a rebrand after our site kept getting unlisted from search engines because it thought we were a cult.

  • We wanted the background to be a slideshow.

  • We really needed a font change since Comic Sans only works on Microsoft devices.

  • The goal was to have the site password-free and we couldn't do that with the old site since it was built around the ancient framework...

Tl;dr: A rebrand was needed for accessibility.

Why is the site a lot less cultish/goosism related?

A couple things actually.

  1. Extreme SEO problems

  2. Juvenile humor that seemed harmless but was harmful

  3. The need for a rebrand (open question one for more context)

Our team unanimously agreed to leave our old branding in the past and go for a modern and mature feel. The original site is still available here.

Can I support The Goose Site?

We do not allow donations as this page is free and open soure on Github. Thank you so much for you interest!

Why did the domain move twice?

First move explanation: The ancient domain (worshipthegoose.github.io) had problems getting listed by search engines so we moved it to the new domain (thegoosesite.github.io)

Second move explanation: We moved the legacy site from thegoosesite.github.io to thegoosesite.github.io/legacy so we could build the new site. For a one-week period (planned to be a month) this domain redirected to the legacy domain.

What if I don't like geese?
