Erduition

May contain religious references. The following is fully fiction and any real events are entirely coincedential.

The Goose was spawned in space at around 9,000 BCE. He modeled and created Earth and all of the other planets in the system. But Earth was special... it had the potential for other forms of life. The Goose spawned in other beings known as Cow and Bull. Once they were created, they explored earth and found a conniving being known as Sinner Shark. Sinner Shark had a deal that he offered to the two recently created beings. Burn the entirety of Earth in exchange for rule on Mars. They agreed and poured lava on the planet. The Goose was outranged so he ripped their skin off and put them in a cage inside of the Earth's core for them to burn forever. The Goose carved and gave life to replacements known as The Duck and The Swan. They both reproduced asexually and birthed The Dancing Monster. The Dancing Monster added colour to the world and then started reproducing until we had over a billion ducks, geese, and swans. The Goose then created many other types of animals including humans. This is how The Goose is so powerful.
